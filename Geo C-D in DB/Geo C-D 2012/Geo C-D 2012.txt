In Communication hormonale we toiled,
Ninety-six questions, some glued, some spoiled.
Headings that clung to the question's own skin —
"Restitution," "Exploitation," crammed within —
We pulled them apart into references clean,
Each passage now holds what the old exam had seen.


Every QCM now wears its own crown:
Bold on its own line, stem falling down.
Fourteen we counted, thirteen we fixed,
One already right when the audit first ticked.

But three still remain in the shadow, unseen —
Positions four, five, six — "Exercice 2," it means,
"Évaluation des compétences" — a phrase not caught
By the regex that hunted what the first pass had sought.
Same glued-heading sickness, same passage-shaped cure —
Find it, extract it, make the question text pure.

And should new content need a birth from your hand,
Check the database first — it may already stand.
Verify every year against a PDF you can trust,
Never invent a figure — pending-label it, or bust.
Fix production first, then staging must follow —
Never let staging trail, that rule's not hollow.
